Logis

Finds the money e-commerce merchants lose to couriers and returns

Merchants upload orders, shipments, courier invoices and COD remittances as CSV or Excel. An ETL pipeline builds one PostgreSQL ledger with a record per AWB. An audit engine checks billed weight, zone, duplicates, RTO fees and COD payouts against the rate card and exports dispute CSVs. An RTO model then scores new orders before they ship, with a rule-based fallback when history is thin. Tested on a 16,000-order synthetic merchant dataset.

02 ┬╖ Spending analytics ┬╖ Personal finance
GitHub Γåù Live demo Γåù

LostMoney-AI

Turns Indian bank statements into categorized spending

Reads PDF, CSV and Excel bank statements with pdfplumber, Camelot and Pandas, then sorts every transaction with a hybrid ML and rule-based categorizer. RapidFuzz matching picks out recurring payments. The React frontend runs on Netlify, the Django backend on Railway, with JWT auth, Celery and Redis for background jobs and Cloudinary for file storage.

Telecom Customer Churn

Who leaves a telecom company, and why

Cleaned and explored 7,000+ customer records and engineered tenure and charge features. Month-to-month customers churned about 3x more than two-year contract customers, electronic-check payers churned the most, and high monthly charges in the first year raised risk. The findings end in five prioritized retention recommendations.
